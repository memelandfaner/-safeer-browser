Safeer for Android TV

A real browser for your TV.

Built for your remote control. Clear catalog navigation, bookmarks and optimized video playback with a dedicated Media3 engine. For Android TV 9 or later.

Key benefits

  • Precise D-Pad navigation: Reliably move one poster at a time (tested on rich catalogs such as HydraHD), without skipping rows or unwanted zooming.
  • Comfortable remote control: Menu bars and dialogs are fully accessible with directional keys and the OK and Back buttons.
  • Powerful Media3 video engine: Built-in support for modern video streams (DASH, HLS) for smooth playback on the big screen.
  • Official TV banner: A high-resolution landscape banner that fits naturally into the Android TV launcher without distorting the icon.

Tested performance

Tested and verified on Philips UHD televisions running Android TV 11. Designed for TVs, projectors and TV players running Android TV 9 or later.

Installation

Download requested. Once the file is downloaded, follow the steps below.

  1. Download the TV APK directly to your TV or transfer it using a USB drive or local network.
  2. Start the installation and confirm the permission in Android TV.
  3. The app appears among your installed apps with a full landscape TV banner.

Limitations and transparency

Safeer makes browsing with a remote comfortable, but does not replace official TV subscriptions or bypass paid content. Protected streams using certain types of DRM may require certified dedicated apps.
