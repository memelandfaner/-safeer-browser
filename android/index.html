Safeer for Android

Phone and tablet, one great browser.

Comfortable one-handed browsing, a responsive multi-column tab grid on tablets and lightweight performance with minimal battery use. For Android 9 or later.

Key benefits

  • Ergonomic one-handed design: Larger touch targets (48 dp) and strategically placed buttons for comfortable thumb operation on modern phones.
  • Smart tablet adaptation: An automatic multi-column tab layout (3 columns from 600 dp, 4 columns from 840 dp) keeps everything easy to see.
  • Battery and resource efficiency: No wasteful processes or constant background polling. The browser runs quickly and preserves your battery.
  • Built-in protection: Automatic filtering of ads and redirects to dangerous sites for a cleaner, faster web.

Tested performance

Natively built and signed APK, tested on smartphones (Samsung Galaxy S25) and tablets (Galaxy Tab A9+). Compatible with devices running Android 9 or later.

Installation

  1. Download the installation APK to your phone or tablet.
  2. Open the file and confirm installation (allow installation from this source if needed).
  3. Enjoy clean, fast browsing. You do not need to uninstall the app to update it.

Limitations and transparency

Filtering removes most ads and unwanted elements, but specific website protections may occasionally require another browser. Safeer does not guarantee anonymity or bypass subscriptions.
