# Safeer Mobile 1.0.12

- YouTube and YouTube Music keep playing: the "Video paused. Continue watching?" prompt is prevented, and if it still appears it is confirmed automatically and playback resumes.
- Push Square, Nintendo Life, Pure Xbox and Time Extension: empty ad inserts and the "Please disable adblock or subscribe" placeholder are hidden.

Slovensko: YouTube in YouTube Music ne ustavita več predvajanja z vprašanjem »Predvajanje videoposnetka je začasno zaustavljeno. Želite nadaljevati z ogledom?«. Če se vprašanje vseeno pojavi, ga brskalnik samodejno potrdi in nadaljuje predvajanje. Na straneh Push Square, Nintendo Life, Pure Xbox in Time Extension so skriti prazni oglasni vložki z napisom »Please disable adblock or subscribe«.
