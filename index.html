An honest approach

Reliability.
No empty promises.

  • Smart filters remove most distractions. We block most ads and known threats, but web systems keep changing, so some ads or special scripts may remain.
  • Adapting to modern video. We optimize playback across all platforms, but external video platforms regularly update their players.
  • Standards and sign-in. DRM-protected video streams and some advanced sign-in checks (such as specific Cloudflare checks) may occasionally require an external system.
  • Privacy without deception. Safeer does not collect your personal data, but it is not a VPN — visited websites receive the network information needed to establish a connection. Privacy details.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or subscription?

You do not need an account or subscription to download and use Safeer. All browser features are free and open source.

Does Safeer include paid movies or TV subscriptions?

No. Safeer is a web browser that opens websites. It does not provide unauthorized access to paid content or replace TV provider subscriptions.

What about security and open source?

Each platform has a public GitHub repository where you can inspect the source code, suggest improvements or report a bug.
